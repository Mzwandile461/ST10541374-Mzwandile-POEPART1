About Us

Our Story

Mzwandile Mobile was established in 2022 in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. Founded by Mzwandile with a vision to make genuine Apple products accessible, affordable, and supported locally.

We started as a small mobile accessories counter. Over the years, we expanded into a full iPhone specialist offering all iPhone models — from iPhone 11 to the latest iPhone 17 Pro Max, including SE series, certified pre-owned, and imports.

By 2025, we have earned a reputation as one of Durban's most trusted iPhone destinations for quality, warranty, and after-sales support.

Mission Statement

"To deliver an unparalleled iPhone buying experience through genuine products, expert advice, fair pricing, and authentic customer care that creates lasting loyalty for every customer."

Vision Statement

"To be the most celebrated iPhone destination in South Africa, recognised for our commitment to authenticity, affordability, and outstanding after-sales service."
